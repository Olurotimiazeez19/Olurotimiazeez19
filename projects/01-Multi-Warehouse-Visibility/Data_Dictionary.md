# Data Dictionary
- `sku_master.csv`: one record per SKU, including category, illustrative cost, demand and planning parameters.
- `warehouse_master.csv`: warehouse ID, name and type.
- `inventory_snapshot.csv`: one row per warehouse-SKU with stock, allocation, availability, value, cover and replenishment status.
- `monthly_inventory_movements.csv`: monthly quantities issued and received by warehouse and SKU.
- Quantities are units; currency is illustrative QAR; all records are synthetic.
